## Shared tips (all portals)

1. Use the **correct login page** for your role (customer `/login`, vendor `/vendor/login`, admin `/admin/login`).  
2. After register, **verify email with OTP** before logging in.  
3. Use **Forgot password** on your portal if you cannot sign in.  
4. Keep the browser session open; access tokens refresh automatically in the background.  
5. Log out from the panel top bar when finished on a shared device.
